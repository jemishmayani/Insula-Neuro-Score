# v0.10.0: Neurosurgical Examination & Grades

## Catalogue
- The active catalogue (`catalog.json → scores`) now holds **implemented scores only**. Under-review cards, badges and banners are no longer shown anywhere (lists, search, related links, Home).
- Not-implemented entries move to `catalog.json → withheld` with their reason (not shown in the app). The future queue is `catalog.json → planned` (not shown).
- The Calculate and Guide root lists are titled **Scores & Grades**.
- Category data may now carry `groups` (sub-sections in the group list) and `include` (cross-listing a score in another group). Search, favourites and recent items key on the score id, so they work for cross-listed scores automatically.

## Removed from the app (withheld)
FOUR, Modified Graeb, ASIA/AIS (ISNCSCI), ODI, Tokuhashi (1990), RASS, JOA, SOFA-2, DS-GPA (reasons unchanged from v0.9.2), plus Sunnybrook (see below).

## New: Neurosurgical Examination & Grades (16 implemented)
| Sub-section | Items |
|---|---|
| Motor | MRC muscle power 0–5; MRC sum score (0–60) |
| Reflexes | Deep tendon reflex grade (NINDS 0–4+); Clonus (descriptive); Plantar response (descriptive) |
| Tone and spasticity | Modified Ashworth Scale; Modified Tardieu Scale (X, R1, R2, R2−R1) |
| Facial nerve | House-Brackmann I–VI |
| Hearing and CPA | Gardner-Robertson I–V (reports both classes when PTA and SDS disagree) |
| Vascular neurosurgery | Spetzler-Martin; Lawton-Young supplementary (+ combined Supp-SM); Borden; Cognard |
| Neurotrauma | Markwalder (also listed under Traumatic brain injury) |
| Functional and neuro-oncology | KPS, ECOG (existing scores, cross-listed; unchanged) |
| Epilepsy surgery | Engel; ILAE outcome (with 1a) |

Examination grades use a new result type, **Examination grade** ("describes what was found, not a prognosis").

**Not implemented: Sunnybrook Facial Grading System.** Item point values could not be confirmed from an accessible primary source, the stated 0–100 composite range does not match the published weights, and translation groups sought the authors' permission. Recorded as withheld.

## Planned (IDs reserved, not implemented)

- Calculation audit now evaluates optional inputs left blank and detects states that share a total.
- Stale "(under review)" relation labels removed from related-score links.
