# Insula Neuro Score v0.10.0: Neurosurgical Examination & Grades

> **Not clinically ready.** For review and testing only. See the medical content audit.

## Install
- Download `InsulaNeuroScore-v0.10.0.apk` below. Android 7.0 or later.
- **Uninstall any earlier version first.** This build is signed with a new key, so Android will not install it as an update. Favourites and settings on the device are lost when you uninstall.

## What's new
- **Neurosurgical Examination & Grades**: a new group with 16 items, each with a Calculator and Guide.
  - Motor: MRC power (0–5), MRC sum score (0–60)
  - Reflexes: reflex grade (NINDS 0–4+), clonus, plantar response
  - Tone and spasticity: Modified Ashworth, Modified Tardieu
  - Facial nerve: House-Brackmann
  - Hearing and CPA: Gardner-Robertson
  - Vascular: Spetzler-Martin, Lawton-Young (with combined Supp-SM), Borden, Cognard
  - Neurotrauma: Markwalder
  - Epilepsy surgery: Engel, ILAE outcome
  - KPS and ECOG are also listed here.
- Lists are headed **Scores & Grades**.
- Examination grades are labelled as findings, not prognoses.

## Removed from the app
Under-review scores are no longer shown: FOUR, Modified Graeb, ASIA/AIS, ODI, Tokuhashi (1990), RASS, JOA, SOFA-2 and DS-GPA. Their reasons are kept in the repository audit.

## Known limitations
- Sunnybrook Facial Grading System is not included (verification and licensing).
- Gardner-Robertson shows both classes when PTA and speech discrimination disagree, because published rules conflict.
- Flagged for clinician review: Modified Tardieu grade 5, Markwalder source paper, clonus wording, Lawton-Young threshold (not shown), Revised Tokuhashi item conflict.
