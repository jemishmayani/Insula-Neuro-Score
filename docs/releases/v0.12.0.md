# Insula Neuro Score v0.12.0: 87 Scores & Grades

> **Not clinically ready.** For review and testing only. See the medical content audit.

## Install
- Download `InsulaNeuroScore-v0.12.0.apk` below (Android 7.0 or later). Installs as an update over v0.10.0 and later.

## What's new: 37 viva-yield scores and grades
- **Stroke:** TOAST, Oxfordshire (Bamford), CHA₂DS₂-VASc, HAS-BLED, mTICI, ASITN/SIR collaterals, Fazekas
- **ICH:** ICH volume (ABC/2), BAT score
- **Vascular neurosurgery:** Suzuki moyamoya stages, Raymond-Roy
- **Trauma:** Canadian CT Head Rule, NEXUS, Canadian C-spine Rule, SLIC, TLICS, Anderson-D'Alonzo, Levine-Edwards
- **Degenerative spine:** Pfirrmann, Modic, Meyerding, Schizas
- **Neuro-oncology:** Koos, Simpson, Knosp (3A/3B), Hardy, Lansky
- **Neurocritical care:** STESS, CPP, calculated osmolality and osmolar gap
- **General neurology:** Hoehn & Yahr, GBS disability scale, EGOS, MGFA
- **Other:** Chiari types, Seddon and Sunderland nerve injury

Decision rules are labelled as such and phrase results as "the rule's criteria are met", never as instructions.

## Not included
AO Spine, EDSS, ALSFRS-R and CAM-ICU (licensing); Paediatric GCS and EMSE (version/verification); RANO, McDonald, WHO CNS5 and the brain death checklist (outside a calculator's scope). Reasons are in the content audit.
